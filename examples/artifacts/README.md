These Python files were produced by live TDD runs of the harness.
They are examples of *output*, not of the runtime itself.

Quality is mixed: tests may be incomplete, classes may be defined in the
wrong file, money conversion may use `float`. That is why the loop
simulates pytest before it commits. Cases 13 and 14 in
[docs/case-studies](../../docs/case-studies/README.md) walk through the
Stack and money leftovers.
